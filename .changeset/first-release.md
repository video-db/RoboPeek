First public release. `@videodb/robopeek-rlds` reads RLDS episodes from TFRecord shards in the browser (stream a whole shard, or open one episode by index with HTTP Range requests). `@videodb/robopeek-react` adds a shard viewer, an episode viewer, a composable player (`Player.Root` and its parts), data hooks and themeable styles.
